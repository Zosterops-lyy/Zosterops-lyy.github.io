Research

From microscopic models to emergent quantum structure.

My work combines analytical reasoning and numerical computation to understand collective phenomena in quantum many-body systems.

I am interested in how local quantum interactions generate universal long-distance behavior—and in turning numerical structure into controlled theoretical understanding. My research sits at the intersection of condensed matter theory, strong correlations, quantum information, and mathematical physics.

Condensed Matter Theory

Microscopic models, collective quantum phases, topology, symmetry, and universal behavior.

02

Strongly Correlated Systems

Many-body phenomena that cannot be reduced to weakly interacting quasiparticles or single-particle pictures.

03

Tensor Networks

Using singular value decomposition (SVD) to represent quantum states and Hamiltonians as tensor networks, thereby reducing storage and computational complexity.

04

Fuzzy Sphere

Applying fuzzy-sphere methods to explore three-dimensional conformal field theories through finite-size spectra and operator data.

Current directions

Ongoing

Spectral gaps in AKLT-type quantum spin systems

I am investigating rigorous and tensor-network-assisted routes to spectral-gap certificates in higher-dimensional frustration-free spin models using coarse-graining and local-projector strategies.

  • AKLT models
  • Spectral gaps
  • Tensor networks
  • Frustration-free systems
Ongoing

Three-state Potts physics on the fuzzy sphere

I am studying finite-size spectra and operator structure in the three-state Potts model on the fuzzy sphere, with the goal of clarifying critical behavior and emergent conformal signatures.

  • Fuzzy sphere
  • 3-state Potts model
  • Critical phenomena
  • Exact diagonalization

CRT Fractionalization in First-Quantized Hamiltonian Theory

Yang-Yang Li, Zheyan Wan, Juven Wang, Shing-Tung Yau, and Yi-Zhuang You

Physical Review B 113, 195102 (2026)

A systematic Hamiltonian analysis of fermionic charge-conjugation, reflection, time-reversal, and internal symmetries across spacetime dimensions, including their group extensions, periodic structure, and action on mass manifolds.

Floating Edge Bands in the Bernevig–Hughes–Zhang Model with Altermagnetism

Yang-Yang Li and Song-Bo Zhang

Physical Review B 111, 045106 (2025)

An extension of the BHZ model showing robust floating edge bands in the presence of altermagnetic and Zeeman fields, together with topological phase diagrams, analytical edge spectra, and perturbative stability tests.

Yang-Yang Li, Juven Wang, and Yi-Zhuang You

arXiv:2412.19691; revised 2025

A lattice many-body study of C-R-T-internal symmetry fractionalization and anomalies, deriving flavor constraints for symmetric mass generation and constructing symmetry-preserving interacting gapping mechanisms.
